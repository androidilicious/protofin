How uncertain is the monthly difference?

Paired block-bootstrap intervals compare each experiment with the original model, using the full common development history.

ExperimentMean monthly Δ95% interval · 3-month blocks6-month blocks12-month blocks

The same contiguous month blocks are sampled for both portfolios. These are unadjusted, exploratory percentile intervals for mean monthly net-return differences, conditional on the fitted strategies. They do not refit models, correct for trying multiple variants, or establish statistical significance. A short sample limits precision, especially with 12-month blocks.

Does credit overlap with ANFCI?

Credit featureCorrelation with ANFCI levelPast-only ANFCI projection R² vs. expanding mean

Projection uses known ANFCI level and monthly change, fitted on at least 24 earlier feature rows. A positive R² means it explains more credit variation than a historical-mean forecast; a negative R² means it explains less. This descriptive check uses revised histories and cannot establish that credit adds no return information. Compare the credit model's forecast errors and portfolio results as well.
