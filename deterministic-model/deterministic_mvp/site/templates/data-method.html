Longer training history

Adjusted SPY, IEF, and LQD prices begin in 2010. Training begins February 2015, requires 36 completed targets, and expands each month. BL waits for 12 earlier forecast errors.

One addition per experiment

The same Ridge → BL → allocation pipeline is rerun for the original and three added-condition versions. Fixed robustness checks change only preprocessing/shrinkage or the inclusion of policy fields. Claims, credit spread, and yield curve are tested separately, without choosing settings for each episode.

Trade after the decision

Decide at 8 p.m. New York on the last trading day. Enter the next trading day’s close and hold until the next rebalance close. The displayed holding month names the entry month.

Same portfolio rules

Starting mix 45 / 35 / 20. SPY 20–70%, IEF 15–60%, LQD 10–40%. Risk aversion 2.5, BL τ = 0.05. Costs are 5 bp per dollar traded from drifted holdings.

How this differs from the original MVP results

The research baseline is a fresh run, using longer price history, exact next-trading-close execution, completed targets only, and imputation fitted separately inside each tuning fold. Daily drawdowns also differ from the original monthly drawdowns. Compare signal experiments with the baseline on this page; the original MVP remains available in its tab.

Data limits and the reserved test

Claims, ANFCI, and GPR use revised histories with availability lags, not a complete real-time vintage archive. The workbook’s policy paths start in September 2019; earlier missing policy fields use training-only medians, or zero when the entire training column is missing. These are exploratory development results. No post-2022 strategy decisions are evaluated. The January 3, 2023 quote only closes December 2022’s holding interval.

These episode labels are evaluation windows, not information supplied to the model. The COVID market-shock window is not the same as the official recession chronology. Drawdown recovery is reported only within the selected window.

Units, forecast stability and audit settings

The combined robust-input sensitivity clips inputs to training-only 1st/99th percentiles and uses the fixed Ridge grid 1, 10, 100, 1000. Preprocessing and target-availability checks are repeated in every tuning fold. Separate clipping-only and shrinkage-only experiments isolate those two changes. The policy coverage safeguard disables each policy feature until it has 12 observed training values, inside every fold. This fixed sensitivity threshold does not certify a stable model. There is no cap on predicted returns. Without-policy sensitivity removes five policy-path fields over identical evaluation dates; it cannot reconstruct missing policy history.
