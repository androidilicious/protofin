Original design

One signal process, three portfolio scenarios.

Summarize economic news

Compare each release with its consensus forecast. Scale the surprise using earlier forecast misses, then form Growth and Inflation summaries.

Forecast and moderate

Use Ridge to predict next-month asset returns. Combine those views with a neutral portfolio through Black–Litterman, accounting for uncertainty.

Allocate and compare

Adjust a starting mix of 45% SPY, 35% IEF and 20% LQD within limits. Compare with fixed 60/40 and SPY-only portfolios after trading costs.
