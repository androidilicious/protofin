Every month the committee sees only relative numbers: returns, volatility, correlation, percentile ranks of rates and of the uncertainty indexes, and basis-point changes. No dates, levels or headlines. The decision is then scored on the month that followed. Saved replay uses gpt-4o views recorded once for the research; the in-browser option runs three persona calls per month on your GPU (roughly 10–60 s a month depending on the model), so start a few years back rather than in 2004. The live gpt-4o option uses the site's own key through a server-side endpoint that only accepts a month index, with a per-visitor limit of a few dozen months a day and a site-wide daily cap.
