Demonstration only. Evaluated on 261 months against what actually happened over the next 12 months, Jev showed no forecasting skill: ranking ability 0.52 (0.50 = coin flip), direction right 56% of the time versus 69% for simply always favouring stocks, and its confidence ran backwards (high-confidence calls right 47%, low-confidence 70%). It still tracked 60/40 in the backtest because its low confidence kept the tilt small. Limit: 10 runs per visitor per day.

Each month Jev reads the same disguised numbers the committee sees and answers two typed questions: the probability that stocks beat bonds over the next 12 months, and a five-way tilt (strong/lean equities, neutral, lean/strong bonds) with a confidence. The tilt adds up to ±3 points to the 60/40 baseline's stock-over-bond edge, weighted by Jev's confidence (confidence 0 → stay at 60/40), then the same optimizer and 40–80% band apply. Jev runs through Cloudflare's own AI binding: there is no API key anywhere.
