Glossary
TermPlain meaning
over casha return minus the T-bill yield; negative means cash would have been better
baseline / priorthe returns that make 60/40 self-consistent under the optimizer's rule; where the portfolio stays if the committee has nothing new to say
confidencethe CIO's one word for how far the evidence justifies deviating from neutral; it sets the blend weights
regime gateelevated policy or geopolitical uncertainty lowers the confidence one step, never raises it
δ (delta)the price of risk; scales the baseline and brakes the tilt
bandthe hard 40–80% limit on the stock share
prior onlythe strategy that uses the baseline with no committee, to isolate what the rules do on their own
