How a forecast becomes a view

Three forecasting approaches, one pipeline. Each supplies only a raw monthly signal: the deterministic model its 8 Bloomberg macro surprises, Jev its probability, tilt and confidence, the committee its CIO's SPY and TLT numbers and confidence, Causal RAG the net direction of its Supported, Contradicted and Insufficient cause-and-effect chains (exploratory: run on a free stand-in model whose reliability checks failed; its IEF signal stands in for TLT). The same learned rule turns each signal into a view, the same past-miss rule sets its uncertainty Ω, the same Black-Litterman blends it with the prior mix, and the same optimizer, costs (5 bps) and score apply. Jev and the committee were re-asked a one-month question for every month since 2015, so everyone forecasts what is scored. Two fund sets: SPY/TLT at 60/40, and SPY/IEF/LQD at 45/35/20 (the deterministic model's and Causal RAG's own setup; Jev and the committee were asked again about these three funds, and Causal RAG needs no stand-in there). Two controls: static holds the mix; the placebo runs the whole machinery on the historical average. Development months only; 2023–2026 stays sealed.

What "direction kept" vs "any sign" means
Direction kept (strict)

view = average + k × (the approach's stated view − its mean), with k ≥ 0 learned on past months. The approach's direction can never be flipped; a signal that was reliably wrong gets k = 0 and collapses to the placebo. This answers "does the approach's own opinion help?"

Any sign (Ridge)

The same Ridge regression the deterministic model uses, on all of the approach's raw numbers. It may learn to read a signal in reverse. This answers "is there any information in the output?" In 2015–2019 Jev's bearish months were followed by good stock months, so Ridge inverted it; that lost money in 2019–2022.
